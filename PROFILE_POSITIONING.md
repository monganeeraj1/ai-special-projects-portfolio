# Recommended positioning for the hiring team

## Headline

**AI Strategy & Special Projects Leader | AI Opportunity → Architecture → Value Case → Pilot → Scale**

## Short bio

Strategy and operations leader with 15+ years of experience across sovereign investment, government, aviation, infrastructure, telecom, and consumer sectors, including 9 years in strategy consulting at Strategy&, Bain, and Kearney. Combines a Computer Science Engineering background with hands-on experience shaping AI-enabled operational initiatives from use-case identification and value case through data/technology architecture, governance, implementation, and adoption.

## What to emphasize in interviews

Do not lead with:
- “I managed an AI implementation.”

Lead with:
- how you decomposed the business problem
- why AI was appropriate
- what model / analytics approach was considered
- what data was required
- how value was quantified
- how model outputs entered operating decisions
- how quality was evaluated
- how governance was designed
- what failed / changed during implementation
