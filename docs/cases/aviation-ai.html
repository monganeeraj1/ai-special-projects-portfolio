How the work unfolded

From an AI mandate to a program that could actually be implemented.

Rather than starting with a model, the work started with the airline's operating priorities and moved progressively toward an implementable AI capability.

01

Align on operational and fuel-efficiency priorities

Worked with airline leadership to frame where AI could materially improve operating performance and which decisions were worth addressing.

02

Identify and prioritize AI use cases

Structured the opportunity set and narrowed it using value, AI advantage, data readiness, actionability and implementation feasibility.

03

Translate priority opportunities into requirements

Converted the business opportunity into the data, technology, analytics and organizational requirements needed to make each use case real.

04

Develop the fuel-efficiency business and value case

Linked addressable opportunity to actionability, adoption, realized fuel impact and economics rather than treating model accuracy as the value case.

05

Shape the AI-enabled decision-support solution

Defined how model outputs would translate into operating recommendations, including explainability, controllable levers, human review and workflow integration.

06

Define the scalable implementation roadmap

Connected data integration, analytics, technology architecture, governance, operating processes and adoption into one delivery roadmap.

07

Support implementation and adoption

Worked across the cross-functional implementation required to move the initiative beyond design and into an operational capability.

Technical depth

The model is one component of a larger decision system.

Actual production metrics are confidential; this monitoring view is reconstructed to demonstrate the evaluation and model-governance approach.

Technical questions I expect the team to answer

Target: What exactly are we predicting, and is the target measurable without leakage?

Features: Which signals are available at the point of decision?

Validation: Does performance hold by route, fleet, season and operating context?

Explainability: Can an operator understand why a case is being surfaced?

Uncertainty: When should the system abstain?

Drift: How do fleet, network, weather and procedures alter performance?

Causality: Which predictive signals are actually intervention levers?

Model

The portfolio prototype uses gradient-boosted regression with MAE, RMSE, R² and permutation importance. Production design notes cover temporal validation, SHAP, uncertainty and out-of-distribution handling.

Decision layer

Prediction is separated from controllability, operational constraints, confidence, value threshold and human review.

MLOps

Feature contracts, lineage, model registry, segmented monitoring, drift triggers, versioning and rollback.

Risk

Data leakage, concept drift, automation bias, false-positive workload, causal misinterpretation and value misattribution.

Value realization

A technically successful model can still be a failed AI investment.

Actual client economics are confidential; the value view reconstructs the investment logic.

Value = eligible opportunity × identification × actionability × adoption × realization × unit economics − run cost.

The important management question is not only whether the model predicts well. It is whether the organization can act on the signal and whether that action creates incremental, measurable value.

That is why benefits measurement, Finance validation and adoption belong inside the AI program — not after it.

What this case demonstrates

The work sits between strategy, AI and operating execution.

Strategy

Frame the opportunity, choose where to invest, define the value thesis and make explicit scale / stop decisions.

AI literacy

Engage credibly on target design, features, model class, validation, explainability, uncertainty, drift and causal limitations.

Transformation

Coordinate operations, data, technology, governance, vendors, change, Finance and executive stakeholders around one delivery plan.

Implementation

Turn model output into a real operating workflow with decision rights, adoption, monitoring, feedback and measurable benefits.
