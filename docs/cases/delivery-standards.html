Delivery standards · reconstructed

What "ready" has to mean before the commitment grows.

An AI programme expands on evidence or it expands on enthusiasm. These are the acceptance criteria, requirements, guardrails and monitoring thresholds I would put in front of a steering committee — the working detail behind the technical review questions in the main case.

Read these as design targets These are initial targets for an advisory pilot — not achieved airline results, and not industry benchmarks. They assume human review of every recommendation. The pilot cohort, comparison method, review capacity and sample sizes would be agreed before evaluation, and targets refined against the observed baseline.
Proposed pilot KPIs

Business and operating measures.

A pilot has to be able to fail. Each measure below carries the rule for how it is assessed, because a target without a measurement rule is an aspiration that gets reinterpreted at the review.

MeasureProposed targetMeasurement rule
Incremental fuel effect Test a 0.5% reduction in normalised burn for the eligible pilot cohort. Use an agreed control or comparison design. Size the pilot from historical variance; report the effect and its confidence interval. Do not extrapolate automatically to the fleet.
Actionable precision At least 80% of the top K recommendations judged actionable. Operational specialists adjudicate feasibility and usefulness. Fix K to actual daily review capacity before comparing models.
Recommendation adoption At least 60% of eligible, validated recommendations accepted or modified for use. Record acceptance, modification and rejection reasons. Review acceptance does not by itself establish a fuel saving.
Review productivity At least 30% reduction in median review time. Compare the same task and comparable case complexity against the existing process; retain evidence quality and operational checks.
Economics for scale Annual benefit at least 2× annual run cost in the downside case. Use the pilot-supported effect, conservative adoption and sensitivity analysis. Show implementation cost and payback separately.
Release criteria

Model and agent acceptance thresholds.

MAE is mean absolute error in kilograms. Assess quality rates with adequate samples and confidence intervals. Zero-tolerance release rules do not guarantee failure-free operation — they determine what blocks a release.
MeasureProposed acceptance threshold
Prediction quality At least 15% lower MAE than the agreed incumbent or simple baseline, on later unseen data. Flag material segments with more than 5% deterioration for restricted use.
Uncertainty calibration A nominal 80% interval achieves 77–83% observed coverage overall. Report sampling uncertainty and segment results; wide or unreliable intervals route to review.
Agent task success At least 95% successful completion on a versioned evaluation set, including ordinary, ambiguous and failure cases. Report each case class separately.
Grounded recommendations At least 98% supported by the supplied evidence on an adjudicated sample. Any critical fabricated quantity or unsupported action blocks release pending correction.
Tool and control behaviour At least 99.5% valid tool calls and structured responses on normal evaluation cases. Zero unauthorised or hard-constraint-violating actions may pass the release tests.
Proposed non-functional requirements

Latency, availability, audit and unit cost are consulting scope.

Latency affects whether a recommendation is still actionable. Availability and recovery determine the operational fallback. Auditability supports challenge and benefits attribution. Unit cost determines whether a useful pilot can be afforded at scale. These belong in the scope, the estimate and the acceptance process from the start — not discovered during integration.

These requirements scope an analyst and operations decision-support service. The initial load-test profile is 1,000 flight records per day, 50 concurrent reviewers and five concurrent investigations — a test assumption, not the airline's actual traffic. More detailed trajectory data needs a separate volume and processing budget. RTO is the maximum target time to restore service; RPO the maximum target loss of recoverable application state; p95 and p99 the response times within which 95% and 99% of measured requests complete.
RequirementInitial target and verification
Availability99.9% monthly availability for the review service. Define the user-visible success condition and error budget; exercise dependency failure and the manual fallback.
Response timeWarm prediction API p95 ≤ 2 s. Standard agent investigation p95 ≤ 45 s and p99 ≤ 90 s under the stated load. Bound optimiser execution and return a clear timeout.
Data readiness95% of expected eligible records ready within 15 minutes of agreed source availability. Required-field completeness ≥ 99.5%. Missing critical context blocks the affected recommendation.
Capacity and backlog recoveryMeet response targets at 2× agreed peak. Process a 24-hour backlog within 2 hours while preserving current decision deadlines. Verify with a representative load and fault test.
Resilience and disaster recoveryApplication RTO ≤ 4 hours, RPO ≤ 15 minutes. Test restore and reconciliation. Use durable decision-event recording and idempotent retries to avoid lost or duplicate decisions.
Identity and data protectionEnterprise SSO, role and source access enforcement, encrypted transport and storage. 100% of negative authorisation tests deny access; protect operational content in prompts, logs and exports.
Auditability100% of delivered recommendations carry a decision ID, data and model versions, supporting evidence, validation status and a review record. If the required audit event cannot be persisted, delivery must fail safely.
Change and rollback100% of releases have versioned artefacts and an evaluation record. Demonstrate rollback to an approved version within 15 minutes of the decision to revert; preserve traceability for earlier recommendations.
Unit costMean incremental model and tool compute cost of US$0.50 or less per completed investigation. Include failed attempts and retries; track p95 cost; account for storage, infrastructure and support separately.
Guardrails and safe failure behaviour

Prompt instructions reinforce the boundaries. They do not enforce them.

These controls sit in data validation, access enforcement, tool interfaces and the decision workflow — outside the language model. Each has an owner, a defined failure response, and a test that runs before release.

Control boundaryEnforcement and failure behaviour
Data validity and timingThe data owner enforces required fields, units, timestamps and source freshness. Quarantine invalid inputs; block affected recommendations. Post-flight variables cannot enter a pre-flight feature contract.
Access and information boundariesThe service enforces user and source entitlements before retrieval and tool execution. Least-privilege credentials and approved data destinations. Deny cross-scope requests; redact protected content from telemetry.
Prompt injection and tool useTreat retrieved text as untrusted data. Permit only approved tools and validated parameters. No retrieved instruction can grant authority. Block arbitrary code execution, external data transfer and unauthorised tool calls.
Bounded agent executionInitial limits: 12 tool calls, 2 retries per failing tool within the total call limit, and a 90-second investigation deadline. Stop and escalate at the first limit; never fabricate a successful result after a timeout.
Grounding and numerical accuracyReference source records and versioned model outputs. Reconcile displayed quantities with numerical service results within a defined rounding tolerance. If evidence is missing or contradictory, flag the uncertainty and withhold the unsupported recommendation.
Model uncertainty and operating scopeCheck supported fleets, routes, feature ranges and uncertainty before ranking. Out-of-scope or low-confidence cases receive an explicit review status; they cannot enter the normal actionable queue as confident recommendations.
Operational constraints and RLOperations-approved constraints and limits are validated independently of the agent. Reject infeasible candidates. RL training and exploration stay offline; only reviewed, approved policies may produce advisory candidates.
Human authority and audit100% human review of operational recommendations. Tools cannot autonomously change flight plans, dispatch decisions or aircraft controls. Persist the recommendation, evidence and review decision; provide a rapid suspension mechanism.
Benefits and release integrityKeep estimated, accepted and verified savings distinct. Finance validates realised benefits and overlapping claims. Version every control, model and evaluation; any critical guardrail test failure blocks release.

The tests that demonstrate the controls

The release suite includes injected instructions in retrieved records, unauthorised requests, stale weather, conflicting units, unknown fleets, infeasible optimiser output, tool timeouts and missing audit storage. The expected result in each case is a blocked output or a routed exception carrying a reason code and a trace.

Release target: all known critical boundary tests pass. Repeat the suite after any change to models, prompts, tools, permissions or operational rules. Test coverage and failure history remain visible — and a pass is not a guarantee against every future attack or operating condition.

Observability tied to ownership and action

Every signal has an owner and a response, or it is noise.

Monitoring should explain whether a failure originates in data, a model, an agent, the application or the operating process. The thresholds below are starting points, tuned during shadow operation to balance detection against false alerts. Model-quality signals use labelled outcome windows, so they cannot be assessed the moment a recommendation is made.

For low-volume windows, retain event counts and defer unstable rate-based alerts. PSI is the population stability index; 0.2 is an initial investigation threshold, not a universal validity boundary. Apply segment and seasonal context before acting.
Signal and starting thresholdOwner and response
Data freshness and completeness
p95 processing lag > 15 min after source availability; critical at 30 min. Required-field missingness > 0.5%.		 Data owner investigates the source and pipeline. Block records that breach their source freshness contract. Separate source delay from processing delay.
Input drift
PSI above 0.2 on important features, season-matched reference, adequately populated window.		 Data science investigates distribution and segment changes. A diagnostic warning — not proof of degraded accuracy, and not an automatic retraining trigger.
Prediction degradation
Rolling MAE exceeds the validated reference by 20% on two consecutive non-overlapping labelled windows.		 Model owner checks data quality, segment mix and residual bias. Restrict the affected scope or evaluate rollback if usefulness is impaired.
Calibration and abstention
Coverage of a nominal 80% interval falls below 75%, or abstention exceeds twice the validated reference rate.		 Model owner investigates uncertainty and population shift. Use at least 500 labelled cases for the initial coverage alert; route uncertain cases to review.
Agent and tool reliability
Invalid tool calls or failed structured responses exceed 1% over a populated 15-minute window.		 AI engineering traces the failing dependency or step. Bound retries, prevent unsupported completion, restore a known-good workflow when needed.
Evidence and constraint failures
Any hard-constraint breach or unauthorised action reaching the output boundary; sampled grounding below 98%.		 Validation blocks the output. Engineering and the operational owner investigate critical breaches; quality failures expand the evaluation set before another release.
Service latency and reliability
Investigation p95 exceeds 45 s in three consecutive populated 5-minute windows.		 Service owner checks queue age, dependency latency and saturation. Preserve decision deadlines through bounded work and escalation.
Cost per completed investigation
Daily mean exceeds US$0.50, or the retry and token profile changes materially.		 Engineering and the programme owner inspect model routing, repeated tool calls and context size. Reassess unit economics before increasing volume.
Operational usefulness
Actionable precision below 80%, or adoption more than 15 percentage points below the pilot reference.		 Operational owner reviews rejection reasons, timing and workload. Decide whether the remedy is workflow design, data, model behaviour or training.
A monitoring alert that does not lead to a named person taking a defined action is a dashboard, not a control.
