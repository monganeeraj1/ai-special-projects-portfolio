A little about me

I started my career in strategy consulting and spent the next decade-plus moving closer to implementation: from boardroom choices and operating models to integration programs, enterprise transformation, emerging technology and AI-enabled operations.

I enjoy the point where a strategic idea stops being a slide and starts colliding with data, technology, incentives, operators, vendors and real-world constraints. That is usually where the interesting work begins.

How I think about AI

Six questions I keep coming back to.

01

Outcome

What decision, behavior or operating outcome are we actually trying to improve?

02

Data

What signals exist, and are they trustworthy and available at the point of decision?

03

Model

Is the right approach prediction, retrieval, generation, optimization — or something simpler?

04

Evaluation

What does “good” mean before deployment, after deployment and across operating segments?

05

Workflow

How does the output change a real decision or action rather than simply produce another dashboard?

06

Governance

What needs to be monitored, challenged, overridden, approved or stopped?
