How I think about AI

Six layers from model to value

1. Decision

What business or user decision needs to improve?

2. Data

What signals exist, and are they timely, trustworthy and available at decision time?

3. Model

Prediction, retrieval, generation, optimization or a hybrid?

4. Evaluation

What does “good” mean offline, online and by operating segment?

5. Workflow

How does model output alter a real process, recommendation or decision?

6. Governance

What must be monitored, approved, escalated, overridden or stopped?
